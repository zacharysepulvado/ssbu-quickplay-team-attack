2. On the PC, make a backup of the existing plugin and config outside the SD card's
   `plugins` folder. Keep your current captures.
3. Extract `ssbu-rule-message-observer-0.2.5.zip` on the PC. Open its `SD_CARD`
   folder and copy the TWO folders inside it (`atmosphere` and `ultimate`) to
   `E:\`. Allow the two included files to replace their existing copies.
   Do not copy the outer `SD_CARD` folder itself onto the SD card.

5. In the extracted ZIP on your PC, double-click `VERIFY_SD_0_2_5.cmd`.
   It only reads the two installed files on E: and checks their SHA-256 hashes.

   configuration match.` If it says NOT READY, send the message before doing

## What the log will answer

The added events are `rule_submit`, `rule_receive_before`, and
`rule_receive_team_stored`. They log only the Team Attack byte and bounded
session-record slot, plus the existing mode/request/timing fields. The existing
rule-selection/application events remain enabled. All paths have separate
bounded capture budgets.

A submit event is a call entering the game's submit routine, not proof of
successful delivery. A receive-copy event is the original B4 handler's local
copy, not proof of sender ownership or a remote console's result. If the new
paths do not run, that negative result is useful; do not repeat the same match
without another concrete instruction.
