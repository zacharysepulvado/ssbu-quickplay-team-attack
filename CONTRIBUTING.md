# Contributing research evidence

Useful contributions contain reproducible evidence and no copyrighted game
binaries.

For an offset submission, include:

- Game version and executable build ID/hash.
- Text-relative function offset.
- A short exact entry signature (16-64 bytes, in 4-byte units).
- A separate direct BL callsite offset and unique exact callsite signature.
- How the function was identified from the 13.0.4 reference.
- At least one independent caller, callee, or global cross-reference.
- The inferred ABI plus instruction-level review of arguments and return use.
- Proof of output capacity, lifetime, and initialization of selected rule bytes.

For a rule-field submission, include:

- Capture sequence pairs for Team Attack Off and On.
- Every other rule/menu state held constant.
- At least three repeated A/B/A comparisons.
- Byte offset, bit mask, and polarity.
- Evidence from a consumer of the field, not only observed correlation.

Do not submit title keys, console keys, tickets, certificates, account data,
full network payloads, or any dumped Nintendo executable/content.

Tool-generated candidate reports are explicitly unverified. Never enable a
configuration automatically from their score, inferred prototype, or signatures.
