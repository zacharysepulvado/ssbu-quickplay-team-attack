# v0.3.0 — Quickplay Team Attack ON

First tested release of the guarded SSBU 13.0.5 Quickplay co-op Team Attack
proposal plugin.

## Hardware result

- Tested in two separate fresh online doubles matches against unmodified
  opponents; neither was a rematch.
- Both matches completed normally with teammate damage active for both teams.
- Match one selected Smashville and match two selected Yoshi's Story from the
  user's modified legal-stage Preferred Rules pool.
- The capture recorded both reviewed selection paths, live Team Attack `01`
  throughout both match periods, and zero dropped application or codec events.

## Download

Most users only need `SSBU-Team-Attack-ON-0.3.0.zip`. Extract it and copy the
`atmosphere` and `ultimate` folders to the SD-card root while Smash is fully
closed. Confirm the game reports version 13.0.5 before use.

The standalone NRO, public source archive, technical research report, and
`SHA256SUMS.txt` are also attached for verification and review.

## Limits and risk

This result covers two matches, not every region, ruleset, matchmaking path,
plugin combination, or future game version. Online modding always carries
risk; this project cannot guarantee protection from enforcement,
disconnections, crashes, or incompatibilities. Stop using this build after any
game update until its offsets and signatures are reviewed again.
